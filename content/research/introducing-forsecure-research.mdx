Security work creates knowledge that should not disappear when an engagement ends. ForSecure Research is where we document the useful parts: technical findings, repeatable methods, defensive lessons, and experiments that can help engineering teams build safer systems.

## What we will publish

Our publications will focus on practical application security topics, including:

- vulnerability research and attack techniques;
- penetration testing methods and lessons;
- secure coding patterns for engineering teams;
- static analysis rules, tuning, and automation;
- project notes from tools and prototypes we build.

## Practical, not performative

We want each article to leave the reader with something useful: a clearer mental model, a repeatable testing method, a defensive checklist, or an implementation detail they can apply in their own environment.

Where responsible disclosure is required, technical details will only be published after the affected party has had a reasonable opportunity to address the issue.

## Follow the work

New articles will appear on this page as they are ready. If your organization has a security problem worth examining, [start a conversation with us](/contact).
